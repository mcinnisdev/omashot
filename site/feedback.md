# Feedback

QACut is built in the open, and most of what has changed so far started as someone saying what got in their way. Bugs and ideas go on GitHub, where anyone can follow along; anything else, write an email.

<div class="qc-feedback">
<a href="https://github.com/mcinnisdev/qacut/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer"><strong>Report a bug</strong><span>Something broke, looks wrong, or does not do what the docs say. Opens a short form on GitHub.</span></a>
<a href="https://github.com/mcinnisdev/qacut/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer"><strong>Request a feature</strong><span>Something you wish QACut did, or did differently. Tell us the problem first; the fix can come later.</span></a>
<a href="mailto:hello@qacut.com"><strong>Say hello</strong><span>Questions, thanks, a story about what you used it for, or anything that does not belong in a public issue.</span></a>
</div>

## What helps

A bug report that lands with these gets fixed a lot faster:

- **QACut version.** It is in the installer's file name, or on the [releases page](https://github.com/mcinnisdev/qacut/releases) if you have forgotten which one you ran.
- **Windows version.** Windows 10 or 11, and the build if you know it (Settings → System → About).
- **Steps.** What you pressed, in order, and what happened instead of what you expected.
- **A quick shot of it.** `Ctrl+Shift+1`, drag over the problem, mark it up, **Copy image**, paste it into the issue. That is QACut Basic doing its job.
