# Which button do you mean

Someone is stuck. You know exactly which control they need and there is no
sentence that gets them there faster than a picture with an arrow on it.

This is the case with no agent in it at all.

## Do it

1. <kbd>Super</kbd><kbd>Alt</kbd><kbd>Q</kbd> and drag the part of the screen
   they are looking at.
2. <kbd>A</kbd> for the arrow, point it at the thing. <kbd>H</kbd> to
   highlight, <kbd>S</kbd> for numbered steps if it takes more than one.
3. <kbd>Ctrl</kbd><kbd>Shift</kbd><kbd>C</kbd> — **Copy image**.

The marked-up PNG is on your clipboard as an image, not a path. Paste it into
the chat you are already in.

## Blur, when it is someone else's screen

<kbd>B</kbd> blurs, and the blur genuinely removes the pixels underneath
rather than laying a grey rectangle over them. A blurred shot is safe to send
to a customer, attach to a ticket or put in a public thread.

## The marks stay editable

Omashot keeps your marks as data beside the shot, with the untouched original
next to it. If the arrow was pointing at the wrong thing, reopen it and move
the arrow — you are not painting on a flattened image and starting again.
